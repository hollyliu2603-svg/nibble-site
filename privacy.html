Nibble

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Plain-English Summary

Nibble keeps your recipes on your own device and in your own private iCloud account. There are no accounts to create, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of ours in between. Nobody — including the developer — can see what you cook.

Who we are

Nibble is an iPhone app for collecting food ideas and recipes. It is made by an independent developer. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Questions? Email NibblesApp@outlook.com.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Nibble — recipes, crumbs, pantry items, notes and photos — is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it also syncs through your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit) so it appears on your other devices.

That private database belongs to your Apple Account. The developer has no access to it and cannot read, browse, or export your content. Nibble has no server of its own.

What Nibble does not do

🚫  No account or sign-up — the app never asks for your email or a password

🚫  No analytics, crash-tracking, or advertising SDKs

🚫  No tracking across apps or websites

🚫  No selling or sharing of your data with anyone

🚫  No location, contacts, or payment information collected

Microphone & speech recognition

The Tell Me feature lets you speak a food idea instead of typing it. When you use it, Nibble records audio only while you are actively dictating and passes it to Apple's speech recognition to convert it into text you can then edit.

Nibble does not save your audio and does not send it anywhere else. Depending on your device and language, Apple may process the audio on-device or on Apple's servers; that processing is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Nibble only ever asks for the microphone when you tap to dictate, and you can decline or revoke access in iOS Settings.

Photos and shared links

If you attach a photo to a recipe, it is stored alongside that recipe on your device and in your private iCloud database — the same place as everything else.

You can also send a link or an image to Nibble from another app using the iOS share sheet. Nibble reads that content to draft a recipe for you to review. Where a link needs to be fetched to read the page, the request goes directly from your device to that website; nothing is routed through the developer.

Notifications

Nibble can remind you about meals and recipes you have saved. These reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Third-party services

Nibble includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries. The only external services involved are Apple's own: iCloud/CloudKit for syncing your private data, and Apple's speech recognition for the Tell Me feature. Both are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your content stays until you delete it. Deleting a recipe or crumb in the app removes it from your device and from your private iCloud database.

To remove everything at once, delete the app and then clear its iCloud data: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Nibble → Delete Data. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to send.

Your rights

Privacy laws in many places give you the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data a company holds about you. Nibble is built so those rights are already in your hands: your data sits in your own device and Apple account, where you can view, edit, and delete it directly at any time.

If you still have a privacy question or complaint, email NibblesApp@outlook.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Australian users: if you are not satisfied with the response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

UK / EU users: you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (the ICO in the UK, or your national authority in the EU).

Children

Nibble is a general-audience recipe app. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no ads, purchases, or social features.

Security

Data synced through iCloud travels over encrypted connections and is protected by Apple's account security, including your device passcode and Apple Account credentials. No system is perfectly secure, but because Nibble stores nothing on developer-run servers, there is no central database of user recipes to breach.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
