Common questions

My recipes aren't syncing to my other device

Nibble syncs through your own private iCloud account. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on, and that Nibble is enabled under Settings → your name → iCloud. Syncing also needs a network connection, and can take a moment to catch up after a batch of changes.

Tell Me isn't hearing me

Tell Me needs both microphone and speech-recognition permission. If you declined either, enable them in Settings → Nibble. Dictation also requires a supported language for on-device or Apple speech recognition.

The widget is blank or out of date

iOS decides when to refresh widgets, so a short delay is normal. If it stays blank, remove the widget and add it again from the home-screen widget gallery.

Do I need an account?

No. Nibble has no sign-up, no password, and no server of its own. Your recipes live on your device and in your private iCloud database.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app, then clear its iCloud data under Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Nibble → Delete Data.
